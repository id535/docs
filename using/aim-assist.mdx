Use **Aim Assist** to pull your aim toward selected hitboxes while you hold a bind. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Aim Assist** tab.

## How to turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the master toggle">
    Turn on the master **Aim Assist** toggle. Nothing in this tab runs until that is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your binds">
    **Primary Bind** defaults to **Left Mouse**. **Secondary Bind** defaults to **Left Shift**. Hold a bind to apply Aim Assist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a profile">
    Choose **Global**, **By Class**, or **By Weapon**. Global is the baseline. Class and weapon profiles override it for those guns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to tune tracking

- **Bubble Size** — How large the aim bubble is around the target.
- **Display Aim Bubble** — Draw the bubble on the overlay so you can see the range.
- **Strength** — How hard Aim Assist pulls toward the target.
- **Stickiness** — How firmly Aim Assist stays on the hitbox after it locks.

## How to choose hitboxes

Enable the bones you want Aim Assist to prefer:

- **Head**
- **Neck**
- **Chest**
- **Stomach**

## Other Aim Assist options

- **Target Through Walls** — Keep targeting when the player is not visible.
- **Include Teammates** — Allow teammates as targets. Leave this off for standard matches.
- **Pause After Kill** — Stop aiming briefly after a kill.
- **Recoil Compensation** — Counter weapon spray while Aim Assist is held.

<Warning>
  Upgrading from an older Aim Assist build to the 5.5 schema v2 can reset Aim Assist settings once. Re-apply your profiles after that upgrade.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Test in a local bot match before you change Strength and Stickiness in a live game.
</Tip>
