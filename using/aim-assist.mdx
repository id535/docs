Use **Aim Assist** to pull your aim toward selected hitboxes while you hold a bind. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Aim Assist** tab.

## How to turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the master toggle">
    Turn on the master **Aim Assist** toggle. Nothing in this tab runs until that is on.
  </Step>

  <Note>
    When the master **Aim Assist** toggle is off, Aim Assist does nothing. Binds, bubble, hitboxes, and Recoil Compensation do not run. **Visuals** and other tabs can still draw if those toggles are on — turn each feature off on its own tab if you want it fully disabled.
  </Note>

  <Step title="Set your binds">
    **Primary Bind** defaults to **Left Mouse**. **Secondary Bind** defaults to **Left Shift**. Hold a bind to apply Aim Assist.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Assist level">
    Under **Aim Profiles**, set **Assist level** to **Subtle**, **Medium**, or **Strong**. That level is the baseline every gun starts from.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optional per-gun or class tuning">
    Use **Tune a specific gun or class** when you want an override. Select **Add**, then search for or pick a weapon class or a single gun. Open a row to edit that profile, or remove the row to erase the override.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The top row of **Aim Profiles** shows the gun you are holding and which profile it uses. The row that applies to that gun shows **Active now**. To clear every override and return to the defaults, use **Reset all aim profiles**, then confirm with **Reset**.

## How to tune tracking

- **Bubble Size** — How far Aim Assist can reach. Larger means a wider pull area.
- **Display Aim Bubble** — Draw the bubble on the overlay so you can see the range. The bubble is red when no target is in reach, yellow when a selected hitbox you can aim at is inside it, and green while you hold a bind on that target.
- **Strength** — How hard the crosshair is pulled toward the target inside the bubble.
- **Stickiness** — How firmly Aim Assist stays on the hitbox after it locks.

## How to choose hitboxes

Enable the bones you want Aim Assist to prefer:

- **Head**
- **Neck**
- **Chest**
- **Stomach**

## Other Aim Assist options

- **Target Through Walls** — Keep targeting when the player is not visible.
- **Pause After Kill** — **Off**, **0.5 s**, **1 s**, or **3 s**.
- **Recoil Compensation** — Counter weapon spray while Aim Assist is held.

## Fast Visibility Filter

**Fast Visibility Filter** is on **Aim Assist** and writes CS2 game memory when TraceShape is on.

- **Enable TraceShape** — Use the game engine line of sight for aim and ESP colors when it is available. Faster and more exact than the spotted mask.
- **Smoke Occlusion** — When TraceShape is on, treat active smokes as blocking line of sight.
- **Status** — Shows whether TraceShape is off, scanning, waiting for a match, ready, or disabled.

<Warning>
  Test TraceShape in a local bot match first. If NinjaHackz crashes while TraceShape is on, the next launch starts it off (safe-off).
</Warning>

<Note>
  Teammate filtering is automatic. In normal team modes Aim Assist targets enemies only. In deathmatch, workshop/custom, and free-for-all, it can target all players.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Test in a local bot match before you change Strength and Stickiness in a live game.
</Tip>
