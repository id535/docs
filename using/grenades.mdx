Use **Grenades** for lineup helpers and an optional live trajectory for the grenade in your hand. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Grenades** tab.

## Lineup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Lineup Guide">
    Turn on **Lineup Guide** to show stand circles and the aim marker. The left color on that row is the plaque. The right color is the stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional Throw Note">
    Turn on **Throw Note** for action text on the plaque. The color on that row is the aim marker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional Metres">
    Turn on **Metres** to show distance on the plaque.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Grenade Trajectory

**Grenade Trajectory** is marked beta and writes CS2 game memory.

<Warning>
  Test **Show Trajectory** in a local bot match first. Memory writes are riskier than reads and can break after a CS2 update.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Show Trajectory">
    Turn on **Show Trajectory** to draw the path of the grenade in your hand. The color on that row is the path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Thickness">
    Adjust **Thickness** for the line width.
  </Step>
</Steps>
