Use **Crosshair** to draw a custom overlay crosshair on CS2. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Crosshair** tab.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the custom crosshair">
    Turn on the custom crosshair so the overlay draws it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the shape">
    Add **lines**, a **T-shape**, a **center** mark, an **outline**, and a **ring** in any combination you can see clearly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set on-target highlight">
    Enable **on-target highlight** if you want the crosshair to change when you are on a target. Turn on **enemies only** so teammates do not trigger it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep the in-game CS2 crosshair simple, or hide it, so you are not looking at two overlapping sights.
</Tip>
