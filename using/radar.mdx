Use **Radar** to draw a map overlay that tracks players and the bomb. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Radar** tab.

## How to set up Radar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Radar">
    Turn on **Enable Radar**. Radar stays off until this is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match CS2 radar">
    Set **Radar HUD Size** and **Radar Map Zoom** so the overlay matches the in-game CS2 radar (`cl_hud_radar_scale` and `cl_radar_scale`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Align with offsets">
    Use **Offset X** and **Offset Y** to shift the radar until it sits on the CS2 HUD. Use **Scale Adjust** if the circle still does not match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Blip Size">
    Adjust **Blip Size** so player dots are readable without covering the map.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Extra Radar options

- **Show Teammates** — Draw allies as well as enemies.
- **Bomb on Radar** — Show planted and dropped C4 on the radar.

<Tip>
  Tune HUD size and offsets in a local bot match, then leave them. Re-check after you change CS2 HUD scale.
</Tip>
