Use **Radar** to draw enemy blips over CS2's radar, or to mark enemies on CS2's own radar. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Radar** tab.

## Radar Overlay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Radar Overlay">
    Turn on **Enable Radar Overlay** to draw enemy blips over CS2's radar. This turns off **Show on in-game radar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match CS2 radar">
    Set **Radar HUD Size** and **Radar Map Zoom** so the overlay matches the in-game CS2 radar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Align with offsets">
    Use **Offset X**, **Offset Y**, and **Scale Adjust** until the overlay sits on the CS2 HUD.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Blip Size">
    Adjust **Blip Size** so player dots are readable without covering the map.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Extra overlay options

- **Off-radar arrows** — Edge arrows for enemies outside the current radar radius.
- **Bomb on Radar** — Show planted and dropped C4 on the radar.

## In-game Radar

**Show on in-game radar** marks enemies on CS2's own radar. It writes game memory and turns off **Enable Radar Overlay**.

<Warning>
  Prefer **Enable Radar Overlay** unless you need the native radar marks. Memory writes are riskier than an overlay.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Teammate filtering is automatic. In normal team modes Radar shows enemies only. In deathmatch, workshop/custom, and free-for-all, Radar draws all players.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Tune **Radar HUD Size** and offsets in a local bot match, then leave them. Re-check after you change CS2 HUD scale.
</Tip>
