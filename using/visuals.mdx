Use **Visuals** to draw player information on the overlay. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Visuals** tab.

## How to enable Visuals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Wall-Hack">
    Enable **Wall-Hack**. Other Visuals options draw on top of that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose teammates">
    Turn **Include Teammates** on if you want allies drawn. Leave **Include Teammates** off to show enemies only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Render Range">
    Set **Render Range** between **1** and **156** m. Players beyond that distance are not drawn.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can draw

Enable only the elements you want:

- **Frame** — Box around the player.
- **Skeleton** — Bone lines.
- **Head Circle** — Circle on the head.
- **Player Tag** — Name or tag.
- **Distance** — Distance to the player.
- **Health Bar** and **Armor Bar** — Current health and armor.
- **Active Weapon** — Weapon the player is holding.
- **Ammo Count** — Magazine count when it is available.

<Tip>
  Start with **Frame**, **Head Circle**, and **Health Bar**, then add **Skeleton** or **Player Tag** if you still need them.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Visuals follow CS2 display mode. Use **Windowed** or **Fullscreen Windowed**. See [Display mode](/troubleshooting/display-mode).
</Note>
