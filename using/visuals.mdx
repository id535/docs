Use **Visuals** to draw player overlays and optional beta chams. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Visuals** tab.

## How to enable overlay Visuals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Wall-Hack">
    Enable **Wall-Hack**. Other overlay Visuals options draw on top of that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Wall-Hack Range">
    Set **Wall-Hack Range** between **1** and **156** m. Players beyond that distance are not drawn. Chams use the same meter cutoff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Teammate filtering is automatic. In normal team modes you see enemies only. In deathmatch, workshop/custom, and free-for-all, Visuals draws all players.
</Note>

## What you can draw

Enable only the elements you want:

- **Frame** — Box around the player.
- **Skeleton** — Bone lines.
- **Head Circle** — Circle on the head.
- **Player Tag** — Name or tag.
- **Distance** — Distance to the player.
- **Health Bar** and **Armor Bar** — Current health and armor.
- **Active Weapon** — Equipped weapon icon.
- **Ammo Count** — Remaining ammo bar under the frame when it is available.

<Tip>
  Start with **Frame**, **Head Circle**, and **Health Bar**, then add **Skeleton** or **Player Tag** if you still need them.
</Tip>

## Chams

**Chams** sits on **Visuals** and is marked beta. It paints players in the game scene and writes CS2 game memory.

<Warning>
  Test chams in a local bot match first. They may stop working after a CS2 update.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Chams">
    Turn on **Enable Chams**. The left color on that row is visible players. The right color is players behind a wall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Material">
    Choose **Material**: **Latex** or **Flat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional Carried Items">
    Turn on **Carried Items** to paint weapons, grenades, and the bomb those players carry. Set the item color on that row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional Dropped Items">
    Turn on **Dropped Items** to paint weapons, grenades, kits, and the dropped or planted bomb through walls. Set the color on that row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While **Enable Chams** is on, **Frame**, **Skeleton**, and **Head Circle** stay hidden. Chams range follows **Wall-Hack Range**.

Chams and TraceShape settings save with your config. After a clean quit while CS2 is still running, those enables restore on the next launch. If CS2 closes while the overlay is attached, **Enable Chams** turns off and that off state is saved. If NinjaHackz crashes or you kill the process while chams or TraceShape is on, the next launch starts that toggle off (safe-off).

<Note>
  Chams are drawn in the game. **Bypass Screen Capture** does not hide them.
</Note>

<Note>
  Visuals follow CS2 display mode. Use **Windowed** or **Fullscreen Windowed** for the best experience. See [Display mode](/troubleshooting/display-mode).
</Note>
