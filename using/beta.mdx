**Beta** holds experimental chams. They can break after a CS2 update. Enable them only for the current session. The setting is not saved.

<Warning>
  Beta chams are experimental. Test in a local bot match first. They may stop working after a CS2 update.
</Warning>

## How to enable chams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Beta">
    Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Beta** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable for this session">
    Turn chams on. This enable is session-only. You need to turn it on again the next time you launch NinjaHackz.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a material">
    Choose **Latex** or **Flat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who is drawn">
    Draw **enemies** only, or **all** players. Set **max distance** so far-away models are skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If chams look wrong after a CS2 update, turn them off and see [After a game update](/troubleshooting/after-a-game-update).
</Note>
