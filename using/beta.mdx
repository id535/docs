**Beta** holds experimental chams and TraceShape visibility. They can break after a CS2 update. Test in a local bot match first.

<Warning>
  Beta features are experimental. Test in a local bot match first. They may stop working after a CS2 update.
</Warning>

## How settings save

Chams and TraceShape settings save with your config, including **Enable Chams**, **Carried Items**, **Enable TraceShape**, and **Smoke Occlusion**.

- After a clean quit while CS2 is still running, those enables restore the next time you launch NinjaHackz.
- If CS2 closes while the overlay is attached, **Enable Chams** turns off and that off state is saved.
- If NinjaHackz crashes or you kill the process while a Beta toggle is on, the next launch starts that toggle off (safe-off).

## How to enable chams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Beta">
    Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Beta** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Chams">
    Turn on **Enable Chams**. Chams use an external dual-pass scene material override and replace Wall-Hack while they are on. The left color on that row is visible players. The right color is players behind a wall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Material">
    Choose **Material**: **Latex** or **Flat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional Carried Items">
    Turn on **Carried Items** to paint weapons, grenades, and the bomb those players carry. Set the item color on that row. Dropped guns stay unpainted. Your own weapon stays unpainted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Who is painted depends on the match type. Competitive and casual paint enemies. Deathmatch paints everyone. There is no separate Players control on this page.

Chams range follows **Wall-Hack Range** on **Visuals**. Open **Visuals** to change the meter cutoff.

<Note>
  Chams are drawn in the game. **Bypass Screen Capture** does not hide them.
</Note>

## How to use TraceShape

Open the **Visibility** section on the same **Beta** page.

**Enable TraceShape** uses the game engine line of sight to filter visible players for aim visibility checks and ESP colors when it is available.

Turn on **Smoke Occlusion** when TraceShape is on if you want active smoke volumes treated as line-of-sight blockers.

**Status** on that section shows whether TraceShape is off, scanning, waiting for a match, ready, or disabled.

<Note>
  If chams or TraceShape look wrong after a CS2 update, turn them off and see [After a game update](/troubleshooting/after-a-game-update).
</Note>
