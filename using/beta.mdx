The **Beta** tab is no longer in the menu. Those features moved onto their related pages:

- **Chams**, **Carried Items**, and **Dropped Items** — [Visuals](/using/visuals)
- **Enable TraceShape** and **Smoke Occlusion** — [Aim Assist](/using/aim-assist) under **Fast Visibility Filter**
- **Show Trajectory** — [Grenades](/using/grenades)

A redirect also sends `/using/beta` to [Visuals](/using/visuals).
