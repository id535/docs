Use **Extras** for movement, HUD helpers, audio, the menu key, capture hiding, and overlay FPS. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Extras** tab.

## Movement and HUD helpers

- **Bunny Hop** — Automate jump timing while you hold **Space**.
- **Head Level Marker** — Draw a marker at head height.
- **Cash Display** — Show money on the overlay.
- **Bomb Timer** — Show remaining time when the bomb is planted.

## Sounds

Enable **Hit Sound** and **Kill Sound** if you want audio cues on damage and eliminations. Pick the clips you can recognize quickly.

## Menu toggle key

**Menu Toggle Key** defaults to **N**. Click the bind and press the key you want. Use a key that does not conflict with CS2.

## Bypass screen capture

Turn on **Bypass Screen Capture** to hide the overlay from OBS and similar capture tools. The overlay still draws on your monitor. Capture software will not include it.

## Render cap

Set the overlay render cap to **60 Hz**, **100 Hz**, **144 Hz**, **200 Hz**, **240 Hz**, or **No Limit**. The default is **144 Hz**. Lower the cap if the overlay stutters on your GPU.

<Tip>
  If the overlay still feels laggy after you set a render cap, disable HAGS. See [Fix overlay lag (HAGS)](/troubleshooting/hags).
</Tip>
