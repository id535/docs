Use **Config** to back up your settings, restore a backup, or reset to defaults. Open the menu with **N**, then open the **Config** tab.

## Backup

- **Export config** — Save a `.nhcfg` file with your current settings. Click **Save**, pick a path, then confirm.
- **Import config** — Load a `.nhcfg` file. Click **Open**, pick the file, then choose **Overwrite** (replace all settings) or **Merge** (keep current values for missing keys).

<Warning>
  Import changes your local settings. Export a backup first if you want a way to undo.
</Warning>

## Defaults

- **Restore defaults** — Reset settings to the product defaults. This does not remove your license.

<Note>
  Config backups do not include your license key. Use **Account** for license status, **Plan**, and dashboard links.
</Note>
