Orders on ninjahackz.com are processed automatically, 24/7. After you pay, you go to the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/) for the download and license key.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What payment options are accepted?">
    NinjaHackz accepts PayPal, Visa, Mastercard (credit and debit), Discover, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Alipay, Revolut Pay, and Amazon Pay. See [Payment options](/payment-options).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does delivery take?">
    Delivery is immediate after payment. The dashboard shows your download and license key right away. A confirmation email is sent within seconds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens after I pay?">
    Checkout creates your account from your email. You are redirected to the account dashboard. From there you can download NinjaHackz, copy your license key, and set a password if prompted. Later, log in with **Username or Email**, then **Next**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is my download and license key?">
    1. **Account dashboard** — [ninjahackz.com/dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Use **Downloads** → **CS2 Cheat**.
    2. **Confirmation email** — sent within seconds of payment.

    If you do not see the email, check spam, then use the dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My email download link expired">
    The email download link expires after 3 days. The dashboard download does not expire. Log in and download again from **Downloads**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the shortest plan?">
    Weekly is £8.49 / week. That is the low-commitment option.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I download?">
    You download a portable Windows `.exe` from the dashboard. If the file is a zip, unzip it, then run the `.exe`. See [Download and setup](/download-and-setup).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
