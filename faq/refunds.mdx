NinjaHackz delivers the download and license key as soon as you pay. Review plans and [system requirements](/requirements) before you buy.

<Note>
  First purchases and trials can be refunded if you ask within 48 hours of purchase. Renewals are not refunded. A first purchase means your first-ever order with NinjaHackz. If you have ordered before, even years ago, a new order counts as a renewal and is not refundable.
</Note>

<Warning>
  A payment dispute or chargeback revokes your license. Open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/) for any billing question before you contact your bank or PayPal.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the refund policy?">
    You can get a refund on a first purchase or a trial if you ask within 48 hours of purchase. Renewals of a recurring plan are not refunded. A first purchase means your first-ever order with NinjaHackz. If you have ordered before, even years ago, a new order counts as a renewal and is not refundable. To ask for a refund, open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I bought a second subscription by mistake">
    Billing mistakes are normally not refunded. You are responsible for not buying a second subscription while one is still active. Check **Plans** on the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/) before you buy again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app does not run on my PC">
    You do not get a refund if the app does not work because your PC does not meet the [system requirements](/requirements). Check them before you buy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app was broken after a CS2 update">
    If the app was broken for a while after a CS2 update, you can get extra time on your license instead of a refund. Open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/) to ask. See [After a game update](/troubleshooting/after-a-game-update).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I got a game ban">
    Game bans are not refunded. We do not recommend using NinjaHackz on VAC-secured servers. Use it in local matches, for training, or on servers that allow mods.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I open a dispute or chargeback?">
    A payment dispute or chargeback revokes your license. Open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/) first so support can help with the billing question.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
    Yes. Cancel a recurring plan at any time from the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). There is no cancellation fee.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Cancellation is self-service. Follow [How to cancel](/cancellation). You cannot undo that cancellation. Buy again if you want to resubscribe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my access when I cancel?">
    Future billing stops. You keep access until the end of the current period. Access is not removed early. When the period ends, access stops.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the shortest plan?">
    Weekly is £8.49 / week. That is the low-commitment option.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
