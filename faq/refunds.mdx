NinjaHackz delivers the download and license key as soon as you pay. Review plans and [system requirements](/requirements) before you buy. The full refund terms are in section 9 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).

<Note>
  First purchases and trials can be refunded if you ask within 48 hours of purchase. The 48 hours run from the time of purchase, and the rule includes Lifetime. Renewals are not refunded. A first purchase means your first-ever order with NinjaHackz. If you have ordered before, even years ago, a new order counts as a renewal and is not refundable. A paid order placed after a trial counts as a repeat order.
</Note>

<Warning>
  A payment dispute or chargeback revokes your license and ends any active subscription. Open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/) for any billing question before you contact your bank or PayPal.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the refund policy?">
    You can get a refund on a first purchase or a trial if you ask within 48 hours of purchase. The 48 hours run from the time of purchase, and the rule includes Lifetime. Renewals of a recurring plan are not refunded. A first purchase means your first-ever order with NinjaHackz. If you have ordered before, even years ago, a new order counts as a renewal and is not refundable. A paid order placed after a trial counts as a repeat order. To ask for a refund, open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/). See section 9 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my license after a refund?">
    Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. When a refund is issued, the related license is revoked and the app stops working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I have a 14-day right to cancel in the UK or EU?">
    UK and EU law gives you 14 days to cancel many online purchases. For digital content, that right ends once delivery starts. At checkout, you tick a box to expressly consent to immediate delivery and to waive the 14-day right once your license is delivered. The 48-hour refund policy above still applies on top of that.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does NinjaHackz look like on my statement?">
    Card payments show on your bank or card statement as **NH GAME MODS**. That is a payment to NinjaHackz (EGM SOFTWARE LTD). Check this before you open a dispute.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I bought a second subscription by mistake">
    Billing mistakes are normally not refunded. You are responsible for not buying a second subscription while one is still active. Check **Plans** on the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/) before you buy again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app does not run on my PC">
    You do not get a refund if the app does not work because your PC does not meet the [system requirements](/requirements). Check them before you buy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app was broken after a CS2 update">
    If a CS2 game update stops the app working and the fixed build is released more than 12 hours after that CS2 update, your active time-limited license (Weekly, Monthly, 3-Month, or 6-Month) is extended by the time the app was not usable. That time runs from the CS2 update until the fix is released. This is an extension, not a refund. Lifetime licenses do not need an extension. See [After a game update](/troubleshooting/after-a-game-update).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I got a game ban">
    Game bans are not refunded. We do not recommend using NinjaHackz on VAC-secured servers. Use it in local matches, for training, or on servers that allow mods.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I open a dispute or chargeback?">
    A payment dispute or chargeback revokes your license and ends any active subscription. Open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/) first so support can help with the billing question. See section 10 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
    Yes. Cancel a recurring plan at any time from the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). There is no cancellation fee.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Cancellation is self-service. Follow [How to cancel](/cancellation). You cannot undo that cancellation. Buy again if you want to resubscribe. See section 6 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my access when I cancel?">
    Future billing stops. You keep access until the end of the current period. Access is not removed early. When the period ends, access stops.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the shortest plan?">
    Weekly is £8.49 / week. That is the low-commitment option.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
