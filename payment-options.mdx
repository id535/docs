After a successful payment you are redirected to the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Payments go through a payment processor. NinjaHackz does not store your full card number.

## Accepted methods

- **PayPal** — Balance, linked bank, or PayPal credit
- **Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express** — Credit and debit where the network allows
- **Apple Pay, Google Pay** — On supported devices and browsers
- **Revolut Pay, Amazon Pay** — When checkout offers them

## How checkout works

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cards">
    Enter number, expiry, and CVV. The charge happens when the order confirms.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PayPal">
    Choose PayPal, confirm there, then return to ninjahackz.com. The order completes automatically.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Wallets">
    Apple Pay, Google Pay, Revolut Pay, and Amazon Pay appear when your device and browser support them. Confirm the wallet prompt to finish.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## If payment is declined

1. Match card number, expiry, CVV, and billing address to what your bank has on file
2. Confirm funds or credit cover the charge, then try again
3. Try another method (different card, PayPal, or a wallet)
4. Ask your bank — some block first-time charges. Authorize, then retry

<Note>
  A successful payment unlocks the download and license key on the dashboard. A confirmation email arrives within seconds. A failed payment does not create an order.
</Note>
