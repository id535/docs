## How checkout handles your payment

Payments are processed by a payment processor. NinjaHackz does not store your full card number.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cards">
    Enter your card number, expiry date, and CVV. Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express are accepted. The charge happens when the order confirms.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PayPal">
    Choose PayPal, confirm the payment in PayPal, then return to ninjahackz.com. The order completes automatically.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Wallets">
    Apple Pay, Google Pay, Revolut Pay, and Amazon Pay appear when your device and browser support them. Confirm with the wallet prompt to finish.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## If payment is declined

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Check the card details">
    Match the card number, expiry, CVV, and billing address to what your bank has on file.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Check funds or credit">
    Confirm the account can cover the charge, then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Try another method">
    Use a different card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Revolut Pay, or Amazon Pay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ask your bank">
    Some banks block first-time charges. Authorize the payment with your bank, then retry.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  A successful payment makes the download and license key available on the dashboard. A confirmation email is sent within seconds. A failed payment does not create an order.
</Note>
