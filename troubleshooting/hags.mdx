NinjaHackz draws as an external overlay. HAGS can add presentation delay for that kind of overlay. Turning it off is currently the most reliable fix.

## Disable Hardware-accelerated GPU Scheduling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn HAGS off">
    1. Press **Win + I** to open **Windows Settings**
    2. Go to **System → Display**
    3. Scroll down and open **Graphics settings**
    4. Toggle **OFF** **Hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling**
    5. **Restart your computer** (required)
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the reboot, launch CS2 and NinjaHackz again.

<Note>
  Disabling HAGS is currently the most reliable fix for external overlay performance.
</Note>

## Re-enable HAGS (if you need it)

Use the same steps, toggle the setting **back ON**, then restart again.
