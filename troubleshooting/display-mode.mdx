NinjaHackz is a GPU overlay. Exclusive fullscreen is supported, but it is not recommended for the best experience. Use **Windowed** or **Fullscreen Windowed** instead. The app shows a banner when CS2 is in exclusive fullscreen.

## How to change display mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CS2 video settings">
    In CS2, open **Settings** → **Video**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the display mode">
    Set display mode to **Windowed** or **Fullscreen Windowed** for the best experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply, then reattach">
    Apply the change. If NinjaHackz is already running, wait for it to attach again, or close and rerun the `.exe`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  The overlay menu opens with **N** and draws over CS2.
</Check>

<Note>
  Fullscreen Windowed looks like fullscreen and is the usual choice for the best experience.
</Note>
