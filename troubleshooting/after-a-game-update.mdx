A CS2 update can change memory offsets. NinjaHackz may fail to scan, and Aim Assist, Visuals, or Radar can stop working until you switch fallback or download a new build.

## If the offset scan fails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use Built-in Offsets">
    When the app reports a failed scan, enable **Use Built-in Offsets**. That uses the offsets shipped with your current build instead of a live scan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the latest app">
    Open the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/), download NinjaHackz again from **Downloads**, and run that `.exe`. See [In-app updates](/troubleshooting/in-app-updates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy info for support">
    If the overlay still fails, click **Copy info** on the error screen and paste that text to support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Chams and TraceShape can break after a CS2 update even when other tabs still work. Turn **Enable Chams** off on **Visuals** and **Enable TraceShape** off on **Aim Assist** until a new build is on the dashboard. See [Visuals](/using/visuals) and [Aim Assist](/using/aim-assist).
</Note>

## If the fix takes more than 12 hours

If a CS2 game update stops the app working and the fix is released more than 12 hours after that CS2 update, your time-limited license (Weekly, Monthly, 3-Month, or 6-Month) is extended by the time the app was not usable. That time runs from the CS2 update until the fix is released, rounded up to whole days. The extension is applied on request: after the fix is released, open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/). It applies to plans that were active during the outage and are still active when the extension is applied. This is an extension, not a refund. Lifetime licenses do not need an extension. See section 12 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/). Release dates for fixed builds are in the [changelog](/changelog).
