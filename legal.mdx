Checkout has one required checkbox: **I am 18 or older, I agree to the NinjaHackz Terms of Service and have read the Privacy Policy. I want immediate access and understand I lose my 14-day right to cancel once delivery begins.** See [Refunds](/faq/refunds) for what this means for the 14-day right to cancel and the 48-hour refund policy.

## Who can buy

- You must be 18 or older to buy a plan.
- If you are 16 or 17, you can use NinjaHackz only with a parent or guardian's permission. The parent or guardian makes the purchase.
- NinjaHackz is not for anyone under 16.
