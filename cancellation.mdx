You cancel a recurring NinjaHackz plan yourself from the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Future billing stops. You keep access until the current period ends. You cannot undo that cancellation. Buy again if you want to resubscribe. Renewal and cancellation terms are in section 6 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).

<Warning>
  Cancellation is self-service and cannot be undone. After you confirm, the plan does not renew. To come back, purchase a new plan.
</Warning>

## How to cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account dashboard">
    Go to [ninjahackz.com/dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Log in with **Username or Email**, then **Next**, and enter your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Plans">
    Open **Plans** on the dashboard. Your current plan and renewal date are listed there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm cancel">
    Open your plan, choose cancel, and confirm. Future charges stop. You keep access until the end of the current period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you cancel

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Access until the period ends">
    You keep NinjaHackz until the current billing period ends. Access is not removed early. When the period ends, access stops.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your account">
    Your account stays available. You can log in, download while you still have access, or buy again later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How to resubscribe">
    You cannot reverse a cancellation. Purchase a new plan on [ninjahackz.com](https://ninjahackz.com/buy/cs2-cheat) when you want access again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cancel is not a refund">
    Cancel stops future renewals. You keep access until the current period ends. Cancel does not refund the current period. See [Refunds](/faq/refunds).
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If you want a short commitment, choose Weekly instead of a longer plan.
</Tip>

## Refunds

Your first-ever order with NinjaHackz and trials can be refunded if you ask within 48 hours of purchase. The 48 hours run from the time of purchase, and the rule includes Lifetime. Renewals, repeat orders, and game bans are not refunded. A paid order placed after a trial counts as a repeat order. When a refund is issued, the related license is revoked. Cancel before the renewal date if you do not want the next charge. See section 9 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).

## UK and EU 14-day cancellation right

UK and EU law gives you 14 days to cancel many online purchases. For digital content, that right ends once delivery starts. At checkout, you tick a box to expressly consent to immediate delivery and to waive the 14-day right once your license is delivered. The 48-hour refund policy still applies on top of that. Cancelling a recurring plan from the dashboard is separate and is always available.

<Warning>
  A payment dispute or chargeback revokes your license and ends any active subscription. Open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/) for any billing question before you contact your bank or PayPal. See [Refunds](/faq/refunds).
</Warning>
