You cancel a recurring plan yourself from the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). After you confirm, the plan does not renew. To come back, buy a new plan.

<Warning>
  Cancellation is self-service and cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## How to cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account dashboard">
    Go to [ninjahackz.com/dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Log in with **Username or Email**, then **Next**, and enter your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Open the billing or subscription section. Your plan and renewal date are listed there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm cancel">
    Choose cancel and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you cancel

- You keep NinjaHackz until the current billing period ends. Access is not cut early
- Your account stays. You can log in, download while you still have access, or buy again later
- You cannot reverse a cancel. Purchase a new plan on [ninjahackz.com](https://ninjahackz.com/#pricing) when you want access again

<Tip>
  If you want a short commitment next time, choose Weekly.
</Tip>

## Refunds

<Warning>
  All sales are final. NinjaHackz does not offer refunds. Cancel before the renewal date if you do not want the next charge. See [Refunds](/faq/refunds).
</Warning>
