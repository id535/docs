You cancel a recurring NinjaHackz plan yourself from the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Future billing stops. You keep access until the current period ends. You cannot undo that cancel. Buy again if you want to resubscribe.

<Warning>
  Cancellation is self-service and cannot be undone. After you confirm, the plan does not renew. To come back, purchase a new plan.
</Warning>

## How to cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account dashboard">
    Go to [ninjahackz.com/dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Log in with **Username or Email**, then **Next**, and enter your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Open the billing or subscription section. Your current plan and renewal date are listed there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm cancel">
    Choose cancel and confirm. Future charges stop. You keep access until the end of the current period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you cancel

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Access until the period ends">
    You keep NinjaHackz until the current billing period ends. Access is not removed early. When the period ends, access stops.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your account">
    Your account stays available. You can log in, download while you still have access, or buy again later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How to resubscribe">
    You cannot reverse a cancel. Purchase a new plan on [ninjahackz.com](https://ninjahackz.com/buy/cs2-cheat) when you want access again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If you want a short commitment, choose Weekly instead of a longer plan.
</Tip>

## Refunds

<Warning>
  All sales are final. NinjaHackz does not offer refunds. Cancel before the renewal date if you do not want the next charge. See [Refunds](/faq/refunds).
</Warning>
