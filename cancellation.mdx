You cancel a recurring NinjaHackz plan yourself from the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Future billing stops. You keep access until the current period ends. While the plan shows **Cancelling**, you can choose **Restore Plan** to renew again.

<Warning>
  Cancel stops the next renewal. It does not refund the current period. See [Refunds](/faq/refunds).
</Warning>

## How to cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account dashboard">
    On [ninjahackz.com](https://ninjahackz.com/), open **My Account**, or go straight to [ninjahackz.com/dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancellation/02-site-entry-annotated.png" alt="NinjaHackz homepage header with My Account highlighted" />
    </Frame>

    Sign in with **Username or Email Address**, choose **Next**, enter your password, then **Login**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancellation/01-dashboard-login-annotated.png" alt="Dashboard sign-in: Username or Email Address field and Next" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Plans">
    On the dashboard, open **Plans** (use **View all** if you see a preview). Your plans list shows each plan’s status and renewal or end date.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancellation/04-plans-overview.png" alt="Plans overview listing Active and Canceled CS2 Cheat subscriptions" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the plan you want to cancel">
    Select the active plan. The plan detail page shows **Current Plan**, **Update Payment Method**, and **Cancel Plan**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancellation/22-cancel-plan-control-annotated.png" alt="Plan detail with Cancel Plan next to Update Payment Method" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Cancel Plan and pick a reason">
    Choose **Cancel Plan**. A dialog asks why you are leaving. Select a reason, then **Continue**. Choose **Never mind, I don't want to cancel.** if you want to stop.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancellation/23-cancel-confirm-annotated.png" alt="We’re sad to see you go dialog with reason options and Continue" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Decide on any retention offer">
    You may see a discount offer for staying. To keep canceling, choose **No thanks, I'd still like to cancel.** To keep the plan, choose **Yes, I'll take it.**

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancellation/23c-cancel-retention-offer-annotated.png" alt="Retention offer dialog with Yes I'll take it and No thanks I'd still like to cancel" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm cancel">
    Confirm that the plan stays available until the end of the current billing period. Choose **Cancel Plan** to finish, or **Keep My Plan** to abort.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/cancellation/23d-cancel-final-confirm-annotated.png" alt="Cancel your plan dialog showing end-of-period access and Cancel Plan" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you see after you cancel

The plan status becomes **Cancelling**. The page shows when access ends. **Cancel Plan** is replaced by **Restore Plan**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cancellation/24-cancel-done-annotated.png" alt="Plan detail after cancel: Cancelling status and Restore Plan control" />
</Frame>

On **Plans**, the same plan shows **Cancelling** and the end date. Other plans are unchanged.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cancellation/25-plans-after-cancel.png" alt="Plans list after cancel with Cancelling status on the canceled plan" />
</Frame>

## What happens after you cancel

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Access until the period ends">
    You keep NinjaHackz until the current billing period ends. Access is not removed early. When the period ends, access stops.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Restore Plan">
    While the plan shows **Cancelling**, open the plan and choose **Restore Plan** if you want to renew again. After the period ends, **Restore Plan** is no longer available for that cancel. Buy again if you want a new plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your account">
    Your account stays available. You can log in, download while you still have access, or buy again later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How to resubscribe after the period ends">
    Purchase a new plan on [ninjahackz.com](https://ninjahackz.com/buy/cs2-cheat) when you want access again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cancel is not a refund">
    Cancel stops future renewals. You keep access until the current period ends. Cancel does not refund the current period. See [Refunds](/faq/refunds).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If you want a short commitment, choose Weekly instead of a longer plan.
</Tip>

## Refunds

<Warning>
  All sales are final. NinjaHackz does not offer refunds. Cancel before the renewal date if you do not want the next charge. See [Refunds](/faq/refunds).
</Warning>
