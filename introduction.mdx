NinjaHackz is a portable Windows overlay for **Counter-Strike 2** only. You buy a plan on [ninjahackz.com](https://ninjahackz.com/), then download a single portable `.exe` from your [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). There is no separate injector or loader to install: you run the `.exe`, and it draws its overlay in its own window on top of CS2.

<Warning>
  We do not recommend using NinjaHackz on VAC-secured servers. Use it in local matches, for training, or on servers that allow mods.
</Warning>

## How buying, downloading, and Validate work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    See the plans on [ninjahackz.com](https://ninjahackz.com/) and pick Weekly, Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, or Lifetime. Weekly is the low-commitment option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out">
    Checkout uses the email you enter to create your account. After you pay, you are redirected to the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Download NinjaHackz from the dashboard. If the file is a zip, unzip it, then run the `.exe`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate">
    When the app asks you to **Enter your license key**, paste the key from the dashboard and click **Validate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The download link in your confirmation email expires after 3 days. The dashboard download does not expire.
</Note>

</Warning>
