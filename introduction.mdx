## How buy, download, and Validate works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Open [pricing](https://ninjahackz.com/buy/cs2-cheat) and pick Weekly, Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, or Lifetime. Weekly is the low-commitment option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out">
    Checkout uses the email you enter to create your account. After you pay, you are redirected to the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Download NinjaHackz from the dashboard. If the file is a zip, unzip it, then run the `.exe`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate">
    When the app asks you to **Enter your license key**, paste the key from the dashboard and click **Validate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The download link in your confirmation email expires after 3 days. The dashboard download does not expire.
</Note>
