You buy NinjaHackz on ninjahackz.com, download it from your account dashboard, Validate your license, then attach to CS2. Weekly is the low-commitment plan if you want to start small.

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or 11 x64 PC that meets [system requirements](/requirements)
- Counter-Strike 2 on Steam
- An email address for checkout and login

## How to get running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy a plan">
    Open [Get Started](https://ninjahackz.com/#pricing) on ninjahackz.com. Choose Weekly (£8.49 / week), Monthly (£19 / month), 3-Month (£45 / 3 months), 6-Month (£75 / 6 months), or Lifetime (£229, sale from £499). Checkout creates your account from your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the account dashboard">
    After payment you are redirected to the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Later, use **My Account** in the header, or log in with **Username or Email**, then **Next**. Set a password on the dashboard if you are prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the app">
    Download NinjaHackz from the dashboard. If the file is a zip, unzip it. Run the portable `.exe`. This is not a traditional installer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate your license">
    Copy your license key from the dashboard or confirmation email. When the app shows **Enter your license key**, paste it and click **Validate**. You get 3 invalid attempts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach to CS2">
    Stay online while the app checks for updates. If an update is available, download it from the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Set CS2 to **Windowed** or **Fullscreen Windowed**, then launch CS2 (Steam app 730). When the app shows **Looking for CS2**, start the game so the overlay can attach.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

- An account on ninjahackz.com and a license key on the dashboard
- The portable NinjaHackz `.exe` on your PC
- A successful **Validate**
- The overlay menu (default key **N**) after CS2 is running
