Your license key unlocks NinjaHackz on one device. You find it on the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/) and in the confirmation email. In the app, enter the key and click **Validate**.

## Where to find your key

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Account dashboard">
    After payment, open **Downloads**, then **CS2 Cheat**. Your license key is listed there. It does not expire.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Confirmation email">
    The confirmation email includes the same key. It arrives within seconds of payment. Search your inbox for NinjaHackz if you do not see it, and check spam.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to Validate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run NinjaHackz">
    Download the portable app from the dashboard. Unzip it if the file is a zip, then run the `.exe`. See [Download and setup](/download-and-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the key">
    Copy the full license key from the dashboard. Include every character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your license key">
    When the app shows **Enter your license key**, paste the key. Do not add spaces before or after it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Validate">
    Click **Validate**. On success, the app continues (update check, then Looking for CS2).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If Validate keeps failing, close the app, confirm the key on the dashboard, and try again.
</Note>

## One activation

The license supports **1 activation**. Stay online while NinjaHackz runs. The app heartbeats to the license server. Failed heartbeats can shut the app down.

Adding or removing a drive does not change your activation. NinjaHackz names the activation after your PC's Windows computer name.

Each activation and license check is logged. See [Support and privacy](/faq/support-and-privacy#what-data-the-app-sends).

## How to move to another PC

If Validate says the key is already on another device, free the activation first. You can release it from the app on the old PC, or delete it on the dashboard.

### Release this PC from the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account">
    On the PC you are moving away from, open the menu and go to **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deactivate this PC">
    Next to **This PC**, click **Deactivate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Release">
    When the app asks **Release this PC's license activation?**, click **Release**. You can activate again with the same key. Then Validate on the new PC.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the release fails, the app shows **Could not release this device. Try the dashboard.** Use the dashboard steps below.

When you Validate and the app shows **Already active on another device**, it may also offer **Release this device**. Click it only if you are moving your license to this PC. When the app shows **Device released. Enter your key to activate this PC.**, enter your key and click **Validate**.

### Delete the activation on the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Downloads">
    Log in to the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/) and open **Downloads**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open CS2 Cheat">
    Click **CS2 Cheat**, then click your license key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the device">
    Open **Activations**. Delete the listed device. Then Validate on the new PC.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If Validate fails

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The key is invalid">
    Paste again from the dashboard. Do not retype it. Keys are case-sensitive.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Already on another device">
    Use **Downloads** → **CS2 Cheat** → license key → **Activations**, then delete the old device.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You cannot find the key">
    The dashboard always lists it. If you cannot log in, use **Forgot password**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The prompt does not appear">
    Close NinjaHackz and run the `.exe` again. You need an internet connection for Validate and the heartbeat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## License sharing

Your license is for you alone. Sharing means any of these:

- Giving, lending, selling, reselling, or publishing your license key or account login
- Letting anyone else use your license on their device
- Repeatedly moving your license between devices that belong to different people

<Warning>
  Sharing can get the license deactivated without a refund. See section 8 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).
</Warning>
