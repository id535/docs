NinjaHackz sells recurring plans and a lifetime license. You manage the plan, invoices, payment method, and cancellation from your [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/). Weekly is the low-commitment option.

## Plans

Prices are in GBP.

| Plan | Price | Billing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | £8.49 | Every week |
| Monthly | £19 | Every month |
| 3-Month | £45 | Every 3 months |
| 6-Month | £75 | Every 6 months |
| Lifetime | £229 | One-time (sale from £499) |

The homepage highlights Weekly, Monthly, and quarterly (3-Month). Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Recurring plans renew until you [cancel](/cancellation).

## How renewals work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Recurring plans">
    Weekly, Monthly, 3-Month, and 6-Month renew automatically at the end of each period. You are charged the plan price unless you cancel first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lifetime">
    Lifetime is a single payment. It does not renew.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Lifetime means

Lifetime gives you access for as long as NinjaHackz offers and supports the CS2 product. It does not refer to your lifetime, and it is not a promise that the product will be offered indefinitely. If NinjaHackz stops offering and supporting the CS2 product, Lifetime customers get reasonable advance notice by email. See section 5 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).

## If a renewal payment fails

If a renewal payment fails, your plan goes past due and your license is suspended. The app does not work until the payment succeeds.

- **Update your card**: Open the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/) and update your payment method. NinjaHackz may retry the payment automatically and email you to ask you to update it.
- **After the retry period**: If the payment still does not succeed, the subscription cancels automatically. To get access again, buy a new plan.

See section 6 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).

## How card payments appear on your statement

Card payments show on your bank or card statement as **NH GAME MODS**. That is a payment to NinjaHackz.

## Checkout and your 14-day right to cancel

UK and EU law gives you 14 days to cancel many online purchases. For digital content, that right ends once delivery begins. Checkout has one required checkbox: **I am 18 or older, I agree to the NinjaHackz Terms of Service and have read the Privacy Policy. I want immediate access and understand I lose my 14-day right to cancel once delivery begins.** By ticking it, you ask for immediate access and acknowledge that you lose your 14-day right to cancel once delivery begins. The 48-hour first-order refund policy still applies on top of that. See section 7 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).

## Refunds

First purchases and trials can be refunded if you ask within 48 hours of purchase. The 48 hours run from the time of purchase, and the rule includes Lifetime. Renewals are not refunded. A first purchase means your first-ever order with NinjaHackz. If you have ordered before, even years ago, a new order counts as a renewal and is not refundable. A paid order placed after a trial counts as a repeat order. When a refund is issued, the related license is revoked. Cancel a recurring plan before the renewal date if you do not want the next charge.

- **Billing mistakes**: These are normally not refunded. You are responsible for not buying a second subscription while one is still active. Check **Plans** on the [account dashboard](https://ninjahackz.com/dashboard/) before you buy again.
- **System requirements**: You do not get a refund if the app does not work because your PC does not meet the [system requirements](/requirements).
- **Game bans**: Game bans are not refunded. We do not recommend using NinjaHackz on VAC-secured servers. Use it in local matches, for training, or on servers that allow mods.
- **CS2 updates**: If a CS2 game update stops the app working and the fix is released more than 12 hours after that CS2 update, your time-limited license (Weekly, Monthly, 3-Month, or 6-Month) is extended by the time the app was not usable. That time runs from the CS2 update until the fix is released, rounded up to whole days. The extension is applied on request: after the fix is released, open a ticket in the [support portal](https://ninjahackz.com/support-portal/). It applies to plans that were active during the outage and are still active when the extension is applied. This is an extension, not a refund. Lifetime licenses do not need an extension. See section 12 of the [Terms of Service](https://ninjahackz.com/terms/).
